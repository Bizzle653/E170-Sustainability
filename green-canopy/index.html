Our approach

Built to explain every decision.

The best version of sustainable investing does not hide behind one score. It shows evidence, uncertainty, diversification, and the tradeoffs behind the final portfolio.

◉Values first

Your priorities determine the screening and impact criteria.

▤Transparent logic

Each allocation includes a plain-language reason for why it belongs.

◒Diversified design

The agent limits concentration even when one theme matters most.

⌁Ongoing alignment

A future version would recheck holdings as companies and goals change.
